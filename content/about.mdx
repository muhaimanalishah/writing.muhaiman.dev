I'm Muhaiman. I build software for a living, and this is where I keep the writing — on work, on craft, and on whatever else survives a second reading. There's no niche and no schedule; a piece goes up when it's ready.

The site is deliberately plain: text, set with care, and nothing that competes with it. No trackers, no popups, no comments. If something here is worth responding to, [the feed](/rss.xml) is the way to follow along, and email is the way to reach me.

Everything is written in plain MDX files and rendered to static pages, so it should read fine in any browser, on any connection, for a long time.
